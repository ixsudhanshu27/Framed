Questions

Everything before you send a photo.

How long does an order take?+

Most orders ship within 5–7 days of AI enhancement approval. Larger multi-photo sets may take a little longer.

Can I see the enhanced photo before it's printed?+

Yes — you'll get a few restyled previews to choose from before anything goes to print.

What if I don't like the frame?+

You can change your frame style and size any time before your order enters printing, from your order confirmation email.

Do you offer bulk or gallery-wall sets?+

Yes, see the "Gallery wall" plan on our pricing page for multi-photo sets with a shared layout guide.
